Card Component

Flexible card with header, media, body and footer slots. Supports variants, padding and clickable state.

Basic

This is the card body. Use the default slot for any content.
Action
 Elevated cards cast a soft shadow instead of a border. Outlined cards use a blue accent border.

With image & clickable

Click or press Enter to trigger cardClick. Padding variants: small | medium | large.
